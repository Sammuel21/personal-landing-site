# Portfolio and app family

Language for distinguishing the owner's work, its presentation, and the future tools visitors may use.

## Language

**Portfolio**:
The personal site presenting the author and their research and engineering work, with the diploma thesis as the main project. It may also be a gateway to related apps.

**Project**:
A body of the owner's work, such as the diploma thesis or market monitoring work. A project may have a presentation without having a publicly usable app.

**App family**:
The intended collection of the owner's web apps, sharing a recognisable relationship with the portfolio while retaining project-specific character.
_Avoid_: Design families, palette variants.

**Design family**:
One of the portfolio's alternative visual compositions, such as Gallery or Eclipse. These are presentation experiments, not different projects or apps.
_Avoid_: App family.
