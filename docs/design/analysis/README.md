## Method and evidence boundaries

Research date: 2026-10-17. Primary sources include the organisations' own pages and first-party accounts by their design collaborators. Each analysis separates **documented intent/history**, **observed presentation**, and **interpretation/transfer**. Company mission statements and agency case studies are evidence of stated intent, not independent proof of company behaviour or audience response.

This is a focused design history, not a claim to reconstruct every internal decision or the full history of each company. Named designers and collaborators are credited only where sources identify them; a historic design credit does not establish authorship of today's homepage. Marketing pages, product UI guidelines, and identity case studies are distinct evidence types. Do not treat a 2013 or 2019 article as a current implementation specification.

The local appendix contains bounded desktop/mobile screenshots and capture metadata, not a full site archive. Text extraction verifies structure and source claims but cannot establish composition, animation, or colour appearance. Failed or obstructed captures are labelled. OpenAI's direct browser capture returned HTTP 403; its current homepage is assessed only for text structure, with visual identity evidence drawn from official guidance and historical collaborator pages.

Analysis text and source links belong in Git. Screenshots, generated contact sheets, capture scripts/output, and working media belong in ignored local storage. See the appendix for provenance and regeneration instructions. Screenshots are reference evidence, never portfolio artwork or licensed reusable assets.

## Research conclusion to test

The useful connection is between **what the work is, how it is presented, and how it changes over time**. Minimalism becomes bland when it removes specificity along with clutter. Strong visuals become empty when they have no subject. Colour becomes arbitrary when it can be swapped without changing the story. These are design interpretations to test with the owner, not measured psychological findings.

The next deliverable should be a small, content-aware visual calibration of one identity after shared understanding is confirmed. The previous ten designs remain useful comparisons; producing more families is not the next success criterion.
