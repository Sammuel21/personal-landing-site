# Linear: specificity through working material

## Why include it

Linear adds a software-specific comparison to the owner's three references. It is particularly relevant to a portfolio that may lead into applications: the product's working material can become its visual subject. It is less useful as a model for the warmth the owner wants, so its aesthetic should not become the default identity.

## Philosophy, people, and change

Karri Saarinen's April 2026 essay argues that generating a polished form is different from resolving its fit with context. He draws on Christopher Alexander and describes needs, constraints, relationships, and visual iteration as part of the design problem. This is a practitioner's position, not evidence that a particular tool necessarily produces poor design. Its relevance is direct: the owner has rejected output that is formally competent but insufficiently connected to their intentions. [Output isn't design, 17 April 2026](https://linear.app/now/output-isn-t-design).

The March 2024 reset account connects product growth with a need to reconsider design. The companion implementation account, credited to Saarinen, Yann-Edern Gillet, Romain Cascino, and Andreas Eldh, describes changes to hierarchy, navigation, contrast, and themes, developed through exploration and feedback. It documents a base/accent/contrast model and perceptual colour work. These articles concern that period's application UI; their type and theme choices are not asserted as today's marketing CSS. [A design reset, 27 March 2024](https://linear.app/now/a-design-reset); [How we redesigned the Linear UI, 28 March 2024](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui).

## Observed presentation

The desktop homepage capture has a restrained near-black ground and a direct statement of the product's purpose. A large application scene follows immediately: navigation, an issue, an activity stream, and a related agent interaction. Small coloured status elements are concentrated within the work. Their visual weight differs from the pale primary action. This is a demonstration, not verified live customer activity. [Homepage](https://linear.app/); [appendix](appendix.md#linear).

The specificity matters: issue titles, states, and relationships give the screen something to communicate. On mobile, the opening still leads into the product, but the dense scene warrants caution. A desktop interface shown inside a narrow marketing page is not automatically a usable explanation. The captures demonstrate appearance at one state, not complete usability or accessibility.

## Interpretation

The hero does not need many visual motifs because the working interface already contains a world: people, records, decisions, and change. It can remain visually quiet without being empty. That is a different mechanism from Apple's physical object, even though both give a concrete subject a large stage.

The danger for this portfolio is to copy the housing around the subject—dark panels, thin borders, tiny monospace text—before there is meaningful material inside. A fabricated dashboard would only replace one generic abstraction with another. The useful move is to identify what is already real and interesting in the owner's work, even if it is only a question, a concise method, and an honest status.

This also reframes “alive.” A product can feel active through meaningful states and visible development rather than continuous motion. For the monitoring apps, dated releases or reporting periods could eventually provide this quality. They must reflect an actual publication process; a fake live indicator or automatically advancing date would damage the desired rigor.

## Lifecycle and transfer

The documented reset provides a process lesson: evaluate real states, explore hierarchy, test in context, and refine with the people implementing and using the system. For a small portfolio, this can be a short loop with the owner rather than a large formal design programme.

Use the thesis as a complete case for the next visual direction, then test whether a future monitoring-app entry still belongs. This tests the identity against different content before building a universal template. Readable summaries and direct sources can carry research depth; meaningful app previews can carry software depth when available.

Mobile should present one legible fragment and an explanatory caption if a full interface would be unreadable. Controls that are only illustrative should not look operable. Family coherence can live in type, spacing, link behaviour, and tone while each project supplies its own subject and colour emphasis.

## What not to copy

Do not import team-oriented SaaS navigation, sales conversion language, simulated collaboration, or a mandatory dark aesthetic. The brand's credibility does not transfer with its visual style. The portfolio needs the owner's personal presence and its own expressive element alongside technical clarity.

## Appendix reading prompts

Look at where colour occurs in the product scene and how little surrounding decoration is necessary. Compare the interface's density at desktop and mobile. Ask whether every visible detail contributes to comprehension or whether some details should become a simpler, larger crop in our own presentation.
